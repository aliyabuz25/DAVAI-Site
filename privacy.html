Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: DAVAI stores your study data directly in your browser's local memory. No personal profiles are created on remote servers, and your data is never sold or tracked.

Project Overview

DAVAI is an independent study tool developed by David Salahov for IB Diploma and IELTS students. Contact: davai.salahov@gmail.com

Data Storage

Your notes, deadlines, and task lists are saved in your browser's localStorage on your own device. This includes:

  • Coursework tasks, deadlines, and priorities
  • Study habits and completion checks
  • Word of the Day progress

This data remains on your hardware and is never transmitted to a centralized user database.

AI Question Processing

When you ask a revision question in the assistant, the query text is transmitted directly to the upstream Anthropic API to generate educational guidance. DAVAI does not store your message transcripts on external databases.

Cookies & Third-Party Tracking

DAVAI does not utilize advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics trackers.

Academic Integrity

DAVAI provides analytical frameworks, criteria explanations, and rubric reviews. It does not write essays or complete assignments for students. Using automated tools to write student coursework violates academic honesty policies.
