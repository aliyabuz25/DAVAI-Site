Academic Integrity Standard

The International Baccalaureate (IB) and IELTS assess a student’s own critical reasoning. Relying on generative models to write assignments leads to academic penalties and fails to develop genuine analytical competence.

DAVAI operates strictly as a revision coach: it prompts you with counter-arguments, highlights gaps in structure, and clarifies rubrics so you write your own work with confidence.

Platform Commitments:
  • No Essay Generation: Outlines, research frameworks, and criteria feedback only.
  • Examiner Criteria: Aligns responses to official TOK, EE, and IELTS assessment strands.
  • Independent Work: Encourages source evaluation and student-led argumentation.

About the Project

DAVAI was initiated by David Salahov, an IB Diploma student at School No. 6 in Baku.

During preparation for the IB Diploma and IELTS, it became evident that personalized feedback tools and revision platforms frequently carry substantial subscription fees that are inaccessible to many students.

DAVAI was built to provide straightforward, criteria-aligned revision support without commercial paywalls or data profiling.
